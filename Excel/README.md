# Excel Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This Excel project analyzes retail sales data from 2020 to 2023 using PivotTables, PivotCharts, Power Pivot, and DAX measures.

The goal is to explore sales performance, identify top-performing products, and understand year-over-year sales trends.

## Tools and Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Power Pivot Data Model
- DAX measures
- Slicers and interactive filtering
- Data visualization and reporting

## Analysis Worksheets

### 1. Sales Analysis
- Sales performance by city
- Top 10 product categories by total sales
- Interactive year slicer

### 2. Sales Trend
- Annual sales trend from 2020 to 2023
- Year-over-year (YoY) sales growth analysis
- Comparison of annual sales and growth rates

### 3. Product Performance Analysis
- Top 10 products by total sales
- Top 10 products by units sold
- Comparison of revenue performance and sales volume

## Key Insights

- Annual sales remained relatively stable between 2020 and 2023.
- Sales decreased by approximately 1.00% in 2021 and recovered by approximately 0.81% in 2022.
- Products with the highest sales revenue are not necessarily the same products with the highest number of units sold.
